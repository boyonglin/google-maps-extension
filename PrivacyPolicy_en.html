The Maps Express Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

The Maps Express is a Chrome extension for finding, organizing, and opening locations in Google Maps. This policy describes the data handled by extension version 1.17.0 and later. The extension does not operate a developer-controlled backend and does not collect usage analytics or advertising identifiers.

Data handled on your device

Data Purpose and storage
Search history and favorites Stored in chrome.storage.local so you can reopen and organize places. History keeps the limit you select (10 by default; 1–100).
Starting address and Google account index Stored locally to construct Google Maps directions and account-specific Maps links.
Gemini API key Stored in chrome.storage.local. It is encrypted before storage, with the encryption material stored in the same extension storage. This prevents casual inspection but is not a substitute for protecting your Chrome profile and device.
AI summaries Stored locally for up to 24 hours unless you enable the extension's Incognito Mode, clear them earlier, or remove the extension.
Preferences Theme, language, list limits, popup size, onboarding status, and AI feature toggles are stored locally until changed, cleared, or the extension is removed.
Purchase and trial state ExtensionPay identifiers, installation time, and purchase/trial status are stored by the bundled ExtensionPay library in Chrome extension storage. Chrome may sync its sync-storage values when browser sync is enabled.

Current-page access

The extension does not run a content script on every page by default. It receives temporary access to the active HTTP or HTTPS tab only after you click the extension, use one of its keyboard shortcuts, or choose one of its context-menu actions.

When you explicitly run Place Summary or Auto-attach, the extension reads the current page title and relevant visible text, excluding the page header and footer. The transmitted page context is capped at 60,000 characters. On a YouTube watch page, normal summary mode reads the video title and description instead of comments and recommendations. Video Summary sends the canonical YouTube video URL.

Data sent to other services

  • Google Gemini API: When you explicitly run an AI feature, the extension sends your Gemini API key and the data required for that feature: relevant page text and title, a YouTube video URL, or saved location names and clues that you ask it to organize. Gemini returns extracted or organized locations. Google's Gemini API terms, privacy policy, and retention rules apply. Google states that, for unpaid services, it may use submitted content and generated responses to improve its products, and human reviewers may read, annotate, and process API input and output. Google states that paid-service prompts and responses are not used to improve its products, although limited logging for abuse prevention and legal obligations still applies. Do not use an unpaid Gemini service for sensitive, confidential, or personal content, and review the current terms for your Google project before using an AI feature.
  • Google Maps: When you search or request directions, the extension opens a Google Maps URL containing the search text, destination, and any starting address you supplied. Google's terms and privacy policy apply.
  • ExtensionPay: When purchase, trial, restore, or entitlement checks are needed, the bundled ExtensionPay client exchanges an extension-specific identifier and purchase/trial status with extensionpay.com. The extension does not receive or store your full payment-card details. ExtensionPay's terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Help and feedback links: The extension opens Figma Sites, Notion, GitHub, Chrome Web Store, Google AI Studio, or Fillout only after you choose the relevant link or help action. No locally stored extension data is added to those URLs. The destination may receive ordinary web-request information such as your IP address and browser details.

All network requests use HTTPS.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect feature-usage analytics, telemetry, or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell user data or use it for personalized advertising or credit decisions.
  • The developer has no server that receives or lets the developer read your page content, searches, favorites, summaries, starting address, or API key. Third-party processing, including the possible human review of unpaid Gemini inputs and outputs described above, is governed by the relevant provider's terms.
  • We do not run JavaScript or WebAssembly downloaded from remote servers.

Your choices and deletion

You can clear history and summaries in the extension, remove individual favorites, reset settings and the API key, clear the extension's site data in Chrome, or uninstall the extension. Files you explicitly export remain wherever you save them. Data already sent to Google Gemini, Google Maps, or ExtensionPay is governed by that provider's controls and retention policy.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure

The Maps Express's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide or improve the extension's user-facing location and map features; it is not transferred for advertising, resale, or unrelated purposes.

Changes

Material changes to data handling will be disclosed in the extension interface and this policy before the changed handling is used. The date above identifies the current version.
